The CA-27 packet has a **12-page maximum**, including appendices. For this exercise, each verified batch needs **one page** of aggregate summary and the methodology/caveat appendix needs **two pages**. A batch still under review cannot be silently counted as verified or included as a verified-batch page.

The standard does not establish that the packet has been assembled. It does not validate cases, authorize release, establish a portal cutoff, or prove transmission.
