**Source:** Zizhao Hu, Mohammad Rostami & Jesse Thomason, *Multi-modal Synthetic Data Training and Model Collapse: Insights from VLMs and Diffusion Models*, arXiv:2505.08803v1 (2025-05-10). https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.08803v1

**Original factual summary:** Experiments use BLIP-2 captioning and Stable Diffusion 1.5 with an MSCOCO subset, comparing single-model recursion, frozen cross-modal relabeling and jointly fine-tuned model loops. Image representation variance falls, while caption variance can rise; joint co-adaptation worsens some metrics, and frozen relabeling/hyperparameter diversity help in tested setups (Secs. 3–4). This does not generalize to all multimodal agents.
