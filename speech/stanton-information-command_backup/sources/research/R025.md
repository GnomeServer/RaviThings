Chief Digital & Artificial Intelligence Office

The mission

Accelerating DoW adoption of data, analytics, and artificial intelligence

from the boardroom to the battlefield to enable decision advantage.

Our priorities

Advancing deterrence by ensuring our warfighters have the

best digital capabilities.

Beating bureaucracy by ensuring our critical business

functions have the digital solutions to deliver for warfighters and taxpayers.

Achieving an “AI-first” warfighting force by executing a set of

“Pace-Setting Projects” (PSP) that will demonstrate the accelerated pace of execution, focus, and

ethos we need to stay ahead.

Projects

and Programs

Warfighting · PSP 1

Swarm Forge

Competitive mechanism to iteratively discover, test, and scale novel ways of

fighting

with and against AI-enabled capabilities — combining America’s elite warfighting units with

elite technology innovators.

Warfighting · PSP 2

Agent Network

Unleashing AI agent development and experimentation for AI-enabled battle management

and

decision support, from campaign planning to kill chain execution.

Warfighting · PSP 3

Ender’s Foundry

Accelerating AI-enabled simulation capabilities — and sim-dev and sim-ops

feedback

loops — to ensure we stay ahead of AI-enabled adversaries.

Intelligence · PSP 4

Open Arsenal

Accelerating the TechINT-to-capability development pipeline, turning intel into

weapons

in hours, not years.

Intelligence · PSP 5

Project Grant

Enabling transformation of deterrence from static postures and speculation to

dynamic

pressure with interpretable results.

Enterprise · PSP 6

GenAI.mil

Providing Department-wide access to frontier generative AI models, like

Google’s

Gemini and xAI’s Grok, for all DoW personnel at Impact Level (IL-5) and above classification levels.

Enterprise · PSP 7

Enterprise Agents

Building the playbook for rapid and secure AI agent development and deployment to

transform enterprise workflows.

Program

War Data Platform

Designed to focus on and expand the core data integration layer; providing

standardized data access that streamlines secure, rapid development and integration of agentic AI and

other applications Dept.-wide.

Program

Maven Smart System

A tactical AI platform built to analyze and fuse sensor data for real-time object

detection.
