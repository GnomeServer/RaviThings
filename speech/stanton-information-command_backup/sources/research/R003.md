**Original factual summary:** The study contrasts replacement and accumulation in GPT-2/Llama-family TinyStories training, molecular diffusion, CelebA VAEs, and a linear model. It defines collapse as increasing error; under CelebA accumulation, minor visual variations still diminish even while error growth slows. It notes contrary VAE findings with another architecture/smaller dataset.

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “the test error has a finite upper bound independent of the number of iterations” for the specified accumulating linear-model framework.
