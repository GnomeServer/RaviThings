**Original factual summary:** Preprint theory studies a categorical distribution on a probability simplex with accumulated synthetic draws and samples from a fixed external distribution under bounded model-fit error. Its Fisher–Rao theorem gives dimension-sensitive ratio conditions for that model; it provides no empirical LLM threshold.
