## Scope and factual summary (editorial, not a transcript)

The August 20, 2026 data essay reports that 10% of a random sample of 10,000 English-language webpages in the July 2026 Common Crawl snapshot showed significant detector-based signs of AI authorship or substantial editing. It is an estimate for sampled publicly accessible English-language pages, not a census of the internet and not a count of low-quality content. The authors describe rising detected prevalence across their series, not measured factual harm caused by AI. Their separate post-ChatGPT publication-dated subset is not representative of the entire web; R012 explains why.

## Short source passage — opening visualization text

> Of all the pages in this sample, 10% show significant signs of AI authorship.
