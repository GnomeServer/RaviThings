**Original factual summary:** The paper’s central theory uses high-dimensional regression/random projections with synthetic data drawn from a shifted distribution and increasing total sample size. It tests GPT-2/BabiStories and image models as well. The “1 per 1000” fraction is a formal illustrative condition, not a universal empirical contamination threshold; outcomes depend on distribution mismatch, parameter regime and assumptions.

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “larger and larger training sets do not enhance performance” in the specified strong-collapse regime.
