# Excerpt record — R032

**Source:** Damien Ferbach et al., *Self-Consuming Generative Models with Curated Data Provably Optimize Human Preferences*, arXiv:2407.09499v1 (2024-06-12). https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09499v1

**Fidelity and rights:** Excerpt. arXiv lists a perpetual non-exclusive license; no downstream full-copy grant was established. Full original removed. The paper was marked preprint/under review in the captured version. See the integrated [research brief](../../research-brief.md).

**Original factual summary:** A discrete-choice/reward model shows how user curation can increase expected reward while narrowing reward variance; adding real data is analyzed separately. Illustrative synthetic/CIFAR-10 experiments include bias amplification under classifier-confidence curation. Reward improvement is not a truth or diversity certificate.

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “the expected reward of the iterative retraining procedure is maximized” under the paper’s reward-curation model.
