**Original factual summary:** The paper’s verifier scores candidates using a fixed base model G₀ trained on the initial real set. Its theoretical results require confidence-calibrated agreement; the small recursive GPT-2 in-context linear-function experiment compares unverified training, a 0.5 real/synthetic mix and confidence filtering. This does not show that model self-confidence universally certifies correctness.

**Short attributed excerpt (Sec. 3, proceedings PDF p. 3):** “the verification score is computed using a fixed evaluator G₀, trained solely on the initial real dataset.”
