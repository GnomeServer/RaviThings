## Editorial summary of release announcement

NDSA announced version 2.1 on March 23, 2026. The update adds environmental-sustainability considerations and an accompanying guide, plus revisions to storage and metadata guidance. The announcement emphasizes that organizations need not aim for the highest level in every functional area. This is program-design guidance, not evidence that maximum duplication is always appropriate or that a particular defense command is necessary.

## Short attributed passage — update list

> Level 4 need not be the goal for all organizations in all Functional Areas.
