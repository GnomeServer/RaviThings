## Factual Summary
AFCEA reports that Stanton discussed a pilot effort to teach zero-trust principles to chief warrant officers at a media roundtable during TechNet Augusta. The article says the curriculum was not yet being taught and describes Stanton's plan to push content to warrant officers while it was being developed. It also reports a separate effort to prepare signal officers in the 26 Bravo functional area as data engineers, and describes prior red-team testing of zero-trust principles at the 2nd Infantry Division as Stanton's account of CW3 Ben Koontz's work. Stanton also discussed EW lessons from Ukraine and education-focused training. This article reports a roundtable, not a keynote.

## Short Attributed Excerpts from Maj. Gen. Stanton
On moving ahead with instruction:
> “We’re going to push as aggressively as we can to teach zero trust initially in our warrant officer school”

On data and zero trust:
> “Understanding the significance of your data is a precursor to establishing an effective zero trust environment that protects that data,”

On electronic warfare training:
> “We’re learning a lot about electronic warfare.”

These are exact excerpts attributed to Stanton by the article. The red-team outcome is reporter-recounted as Stanton's account of Koontz's earlier pilot; it is not represented here as an independently verified operational result.
