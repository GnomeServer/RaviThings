## Factual Summary
GovCIO Media covers a media roundtable with Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton on June 2, 2026 at AFCEA TechNet Cyber in Baltimore, Maryland. Stanton articulated a fundamental shift in defense contracting culture, stating that commercial contractors supporting defense networks must be treated and held accountable as integral members of the warfighting team, rather than through traditional arm's-length engagements. He argued that the defense information network is too vast, interconnected, and reliant on commercial technology for any single entity to defend in isolation. Under this approach, contractor personnel must be trained to common operational weapon-system standards, not merely their company's proprietary product. Stanton emphasized that this accountability is driven by the rapid emergence of frontier artificial intelligence models, which allow adversaries to discover and chain vulnerabilities at unprecedented speed and scale, creating intense race conditions to patch and defend.
