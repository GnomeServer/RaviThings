## Factual Summary
DefenseScoop reports on Stanton's remarks to an audience at the annual Billington CyberSecurity Summit in Washington. Stanton argued that deferred network and data sustainment had created risk, urged treating networks and data in a weapon-system readiness context, and said operators need to understand the systems and agent behavior before using agents on networks for which he is responsible. He described the potential consequences of an unexplained configuration change to communications supporting troops in contact as unacceptable. The article also includes the reporter's broader framing, other organizations' actions, and material about outside companies; those are not Stanton's statements. Stanton did not report an incident on a network for which he is responsible or state a general refusal to use agents.
