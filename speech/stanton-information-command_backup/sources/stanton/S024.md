## Factual Summary
Jason Miller reports Stanton's June 2 speech and subsequent reporter briefing. Stanton links interdependent technology systems to teamwork across DISA and its industry partners. He argues for integrated, mission-relevant delivery rather than isolated components, and connects accountability with clear expectations, training, qualification, and leadership development.
