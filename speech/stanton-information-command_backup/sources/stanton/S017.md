## Factual Summary
The article reports from the Cyber Workforce Summit in Washington; its body explicitly says top officials spoke on Tuesday and the article identifies the summit's first day as Tuesday, March 24. Stanton described cyber warfighters' daily contact with adversaries and called force development and skills validation readiness issues. He discussed zero-trust principles, data engineering and automation, and described a faster “surgical hiring” approach. The article attributes Cybercom 2.0 talent-management proposals to Sutton, including “domain mastery,” and private-sector compensation and retention comments to Vice Adm. Berg. Those remarks are not Stanton's.
