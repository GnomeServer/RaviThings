## Factual Summary
GovCIO Media covers statements by Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton during a January 13, 2025 media roundtable at Fort Meade and the January 15, 2025 JFHQ-DODIN 10th anniversary event. Marking a decade of operations, Stanton announced an operational inflection point and previewed the command's "How We Prevail" strategic plan. The strategy shifts cyber defense from a reactive posture to a proactive threat mitigation and cost-imposition model. Stanton explained that the command cannot effectively manage 3.5 million endpoints independently; instead, under the DODIN Command Operational Framework execution order, operational commanders across the Department are empowered and held accountable for cyber defense within their respective areas of operation. Stanton emphasized mission-driven data analytics, focusing on collecting relevant data to answer specific operational questions and generate actionable cyber tasking orders rather than passively reacting to alerts.
