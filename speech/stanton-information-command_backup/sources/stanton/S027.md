## Factual Summary
MeriTalk reports Stanton's September 10 remarks at the 17th Annual Billington CyberSecurity Summit. He calls for applying weapon-system maintenance and readiness practices to networks and data, and for personnel to periodically demonstrate their ability to perform assigned tasks. The article presents his assessment of AI's effect on cybersecurity; it does not discuss recursive-training model collapse or the proposed command.
