## Factual Summary
The article reports Stanton's Senate testimony about civilian workforce losses, realignment toward evolving missions, and a request for targeted hiring. His cited priorities include the multi-partner environment, DoDNet, data analysis and engineering, network routing, and cybersecurity skills. Separate workforce statistics and automation comments from other officials are not treated as Stanton's statements.
