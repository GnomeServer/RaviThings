## Factual Summary
SIGNAL Media reports on an in-depth interview with Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton regarding DISA's multi-partner environment (MPE) implementation, zero-trust expansion via Thunderdome, and workforce training. Stanton detailed how DCDC campaigns against adversaries by identifying correlations across threat activity rather than viewing incidents in isolation. He explained that MPE replaces rigid, hardware-defined coalition networks with dynamic, data-centric zero-trust access controls that allow U.S. forces to securely share classified data with shifting multinational partners. Stanton described the qualification program for Cybersecurity Service Providers (CSSPs), emphasizing that zero trust is an operational weapon system requiring rigorous team qualification in simulated, AI-informed environments.
