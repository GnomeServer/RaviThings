## Factual Summary
The article reports on Stanton's opening remarks at the 2025 DoD Cyber Workforce Summit at National Defense University at Fort McNair, Washington, D.C. Stanton called for technical competence, lifelong learning, and a warrior ethos among cybersecurity personnel. He urged commanders to understand battlefield data flows, described foundational technical skills, and said cybersecurity specialists should anticipate threats rather than wait for alerts. The article separately quotes Ashley Manning; no Manning wording is attributed to Stanton here.

## Short Attributed Style-Anchor Excerpts

### Commander Responsibility
> “Shame on every commander and leader that doesn't understand how data flows on the battlefield.”

### Technical Aptitude
> “You’ve got to understand operating systems, you’ve got to know how to write code, and you’ve got to understand networks.”

### Anticipating Threats
> “If you're a cybersecurity expert and you're waiting for your tool to blink an alert, you're too slow, you're too late”
