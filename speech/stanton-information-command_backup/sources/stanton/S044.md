## Factual Summary
Defense One reports on an interview with Maj. Gen. Paul Stanton as part of its "State of Defense" series. Stanton identified electronic warfare (EW) as the Cyber Center of Excellence's top priority for combat training in 2024. Following a 120-day Army study evaluating cyber and EW resources, Stanton outlined the urgent requirement to field two-soldier dismounted EW "Manpack" systems to brigade combat teams. These systems allow soldiers to geolocate adversary signals and jam enemy communications, but Stanton emphasized an equally critical role: turning the system back onto friendly formations to understand their own electromagnetic signatures. Stanton argued that exposing maneuver commanders to contested spectrum environments only at Combat Training Centers is "way too late"; commanders must experience jamming during home-station training at installations like Fort Campbell, Fort Liberty, and Fort Drum to develop noise discipline, signature management, and tactics before deployment.
