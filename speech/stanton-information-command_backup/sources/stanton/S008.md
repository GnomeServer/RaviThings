## Factual Summary
The article reports Stanton's keynote at TechNet Cyber 2026 in Baltimore on June 2. Stanton said frontier models were changing how people think and the operating environment. The article describes vulnerability discovery and exploitation as a challenge associated with frontier AI, then quotes Stanton saying his team alone could not execute the full remediation chain at speed and calling for government-industry partnership. The article attributes no claim about recursive training, synthetic data, or model collapse to him.
