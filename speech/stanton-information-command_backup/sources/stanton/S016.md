## Factual Summary
DefenseScoop reports that JFHQ-DODIN was using a new DOD Information Network command-operations framework to align 46 network areas of operation with commanders and directors. Stanton said this changes JFHQ-DODIN's work from attempting to independently manage 3.5 million endpoints to operating with effective area leaders. He also emphasized doctrine, readiness, training, data use, and commander accountability. The reporting describes JFHQ-DODIN's 10th anniversary as Wednesday; it does not establish the precise day of Stanton's interview, which was described as occurring “this week.”
