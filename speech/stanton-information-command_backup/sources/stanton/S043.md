## Factual Summary
DefenseScoop reports on the July 2, 2024 AUSA symposium on cyber and information advantage. The article highlights presentations by Lt. Gen. Maria Barrett (Commander, Army Cyber Command) and Maj. Gen. Paul Stanton on the requirement to operate continuously in the "competition phase"—the operational space below the threshold of armed conflict. Stanton emphasized that cyber and information operations cannot be proposed to geographic combatant commanders in abstract or generic terms. Instead, proponents must do rigorous homework on the front end, defining specific objectives that nest directly into the combatant commander's campaign plan. Stanton also discussed the operational workflow of Theater Information Advantage Detachments (TIADs) and highlighted the future necessity of negotiating information-sharing agreements with host nations to account for theater mission dependencies.
