## Factual Summary
Nextgov covers the 2020 AFCEA Army Signal Conference, where military officials urged defense contractors to prioritize architectural integration and foundational cybersecurity over standalone, proprietary solutions. Brig. Gen. Paul Stanton, speaking as deputy director of operations for U.S. Cyber Command on a panel addressing real-time battlefield data delivery, strongly rejected bespoke gadgets that fail to interoperate with existing systems. Stanton emphasized that creating an information advantage faster than adversaries is "warfighting 101," but requires commercial developers to design components from inception to be integrated seamlessly into broader military networks rather than adding administrative complexity.

## Short Attributed Style-Anchor Excerpts

### Rejecting Bespoke Gadgets
> “Don't give me a bespoke, unique solution for one small problem, so that I can add it to the 42 other devices that I'm hooking up. That’s not helpful.”

### Designing for Integration
> “How do I integrate these components? How do I design them such that they are intended to be integrated? These are some of the challenges that we need our industry partners to help us with.”

### Information Advantage as Core Warfighting
> Stanton argued that figuring out how to create an information advantage faster than opponents is “warfighting 101.”
