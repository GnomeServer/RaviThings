- **Event notices:** S063 and S071 establish program/speaker leads, not delivered words. No accessible substantive transcript of those particular addresses was located in the recorded follow-ups; this is not a claim that none exists anywhere.
- **Recovered policy material:** the previously blocked AI strategy, adoption strategy, metadata guidance, records strategy and joint provenance guidance were recovered through authorized government-document repositories or ordinary public access. They are not silently treated as still-unread sources.
- **Scientific scope:** controlled recursive-training effects, detector-based web measurements and preservation practices do not establish inevitable internet-wide collapse, a deadline, a measured superintelligence outcome, or the organizational superiority of a new command. Preserving origins and bytes does not certify factual truth or model fitness.
- **Coverage:** the search follows the named public source families, topics, career periods and recorded citation/event links. It does not claim access to private/classified material or every possible speech or paper. No bulk internet training dataset was collected.
