## Editorial summary — Methods and Results

The self-published Graphite study samples 55,400 Common Crawl URLs restricted to English-language articles/listicles with article-schema markup, at least 100 words and publication dates between January 2020 and March 2026. Its reported Q1 2026 estimate is 49.9% primarily AI-generated. It averages quarterly estimates from Pangram, Copyleaks and GPTZero; the binary transformations are not identical: Pangram combines AI and AI-assisted proportions, Copyleaks compares AI versus human proportions, and GPTZero counts both AI and Mixed labels as primarily AI.

## Limitations

The authors did not test detector accuracy on AI drafts edited or rewritten by humans. Their false-negative checks generated articles with three specified models; those results cannot establish accuracy on every generator or real-world hybrid article. Dates, schema requirements, genre, sample timing, and detector thresholds differ from Pew's July 2026 page sample. These estimates are not interchangeable and do not prove that half the internet is false or low quality.
