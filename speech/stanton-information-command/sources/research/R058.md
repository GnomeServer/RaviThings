# Excerpt record — R058

**Source:** Lianghe Shi et al., *A Closer Look at Model Collapse: From a Generalization-to-Memorization Perspective*, arXiv:2509.16499v3 (2025-12-25). https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.16499v3

**Fidelity and rights:** Excerpt. arXiv lists a perpetual non-exclusive license; no downstream full-copy grant was established. Full original removed. See the integrated [research brief](../../research-brief.md).

**Original factual summary:** A roughly 16M-parameter DDPM/UNet is recursively trained on CIFAR-10 subsets under replace and accumulate-subsample paradigms. The authors track nearest-training-image generalization score and estimated data entropy; in the studied image settings both collapse and memorization increase, and entropy-selection strategies improve FID/generalization. The authors note they do not yet explain why entropy declines; greedy selection is computationally costly (Secs. 3–6, pp. 5–13).

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “a transition from generalization to memorization during model collapse in diffusion models.”
