**Source:** Martin Briesch, Dominik Sobania & Franz Rothlauf, *Large Language Models Suffer From Their Own Output: An Analysis of the Self-Consuming Training Loop*, arXiv:2311.16822v2 (2024-06-17). https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16822v2

**Fidelity and rights:** Excerpt. arXiv’s perpetual non-exclusive distribution license is not an identified downstream reuse grant; full original removed. Methods/results were read before conversion. See the integrated [research brief](../../research-brief.md).

**Original factual summary:** Uses a GPT-style model trained from scratch on a checkable logic-expression task, not natural web text. It separately measures correctness and diversity across recursive cycles. In the studied artificial task, correctness may remain while diversity falls; added fresh data slows, but does not stop, the decline.

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “Fresh data can slow down this decline, but not stop it.”
