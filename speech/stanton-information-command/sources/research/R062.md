**Original factual summary:** Learning-theory paper models sequential tasks with natural/synthetic mixtures and unknown example provenance. It analyzes mean estimation and PAC learning: uniform ERM weighting is not always variance-optimal or consistent, while proposed generation-aware algorithms can converge under the paper’s conditions. It is not empirical LLM evidence.
