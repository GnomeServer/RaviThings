**Summary:** The paper formalizes a boosting-inspired process that combines model-generated responses with exogenous weak labels and directs annotation toward difficult prompts. Its theory assumes a strong learner that can match the input conditional distribution, an efficiently and unambiguously evaluable binary quality function, and a weak labeler with a positive fraction of correct responses (arXiv v1, §§4.1–4.2, Definitions 1–3; Theorem 6). These conditions are material: the result is not autonomous self-improvement without external information or a guarantee for arbitrary weak data and arbitrary models. The paper also reports task-specific experiments; no numerical generalization is adopted here.
