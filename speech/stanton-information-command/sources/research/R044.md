It applied Surfer detection to 500-word chunks and treated articles with a majority of detected AI content as AI-generated. ## Evidence limits and conflict resolution

This is a commercial firm's self-published study, not a peer-reviewed paper. A Futurism report used a 65,000 denominator; the primary study as actually read says 43,000. Use the primary methods and do not silently reconcile the discrepancy. Neither denominator describes all internet pages. The current page is a live, superseded version; the package does not claim to reconstruct exactly what the October 2025 page showed at publication. No bulk article dataset was collected.
