**Original factual summary:** Under regularity and smoothness assumptions, accumulated-data iterative MLE has a consistency guarantee even as the real-data fraction vanishes; constructed counterexamples collapse when those assumptions fail. The theorem is conditional statistical theory, not a frontier-LLM experiment.

**Short attributed excerpt (Introduction, proceedings PDF p. 76814):** “there exist models that can suffer from severe model collapse even in such a setting.”
