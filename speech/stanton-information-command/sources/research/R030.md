# Excerpt record — R030

**Source:** Ryuichiro Hataya, Han Bao & Hiromi Arai, *Will Large-scale Generative Models Corrupt Future Datasets?*, ICCV 2023. https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023/html/Hataya_Will_Large-scale_Generative_Models_Corrupt_Future_Datasets_ICCV_2023_paper.html

**Fidelity and rights:** Excerpt. CVF labels its copy an open-access version, but its policy says the copyright holder’s terms govern reuse; no separate reuse license for this paper was established. Full original removed. See the integrated [research brief](../../research-brief.md).

**Original factual summary:** Simulates image-dataset contamination by replacing 20%, 40% or 80% of ImageNet/COCO images with Stable Diffusion outputs and measures downstream tasks. For ImageNet classification, declines are described as marginal up to 40% and notable at 80% in tested models. This is not recursive language-model evidence or a general contamination threshold.

**Short attributed excerpt (Abstract, PDF p. 1):** “generated images negatively affect downstream performance, while the significance depends on tasks and the amount of generated images.”
