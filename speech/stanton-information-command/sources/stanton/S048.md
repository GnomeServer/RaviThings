## Factual Summary
MeriTalk covers remarks by Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton at the Billington Cybersecurity Summit on September 11, 2025. Stanton discussed DISA's accelerated implementation of the Multi-Partner Environment (MPE), a hybrid-cloud architecture designed to provide U.S. and multinational coalition forces with real-time access to shared mission data. Stanton explained that modern conflicts will require rapidly shifting multinational coalitions that cannot rely on legacy, hardware-based cross-domain guard solutions. Instead, DISA is leveraging hybrid cloud, enterprise identity, credential, and access management (ICAM), and zero-trust principles to establish fine-grained, data-layer access controls. This architecture achieves a manageable degree of operational risk while enabling mission data integration across combatant commands without siloed service-specific barriers.

## Short Attributed Style-Anchor Excerpts

### Multi-Partner Environment Prioritization
> “I’m super excited about this – it’s increasingly relevant, important, and [plays a] significant role in development, designing, operating, maintaining, and defending the multi-partner environment.”

### Seamless Data Integration for Coalitions
> “Our partners have to seamlessly integrate their data into the operating environment so that the warfighting community can digest, respond, operate, feed back into that look. It is a critical responsibility, and one in which we are prioritizing so that we can apply the latest and newest technologies in a meaningful way.”

### Managing Risk at Mission Speed
> Stanton explained that this approach meets security requirements for a “manageable degree of risk” that allows DISA to “operate critical to the design” at mission speed.

### Rejecting Siloed Service Architectures
> “If we have independent services providing independent solutions to the different combatant commands, it won't work. We're prepared to move and we're excited about the opportunity.”
