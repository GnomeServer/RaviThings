## Factual Summary
AFCEA reports the Army planned to expose soldiers across military occupational specialties to electromagnetic warfare (EW) training at their home stations. Stanton described a pilot with the Maneuver Center of Excellence exposing infantry and armor students to jamming and geolocation effects, and said the intent was to expand the training through professional military education. He noted the need for controlled, authorized spectrum use at home stations and said Fort Irwin training lessons should be brought to home stations. The report separately attributes details about training areas and facilities to Boudreau.
