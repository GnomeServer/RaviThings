## Overview & Context
Official Army coverage of the revamp of Field Manual (FM) 3-12 (Cyberspace and Electronic Warfare Operations). Stanton, then serving as Brigadier General and Commander of the Cyber Center of Excellence at Fort Gordon, detailed how the doctrine integrates cyber operations into multi-domain operations.

## Speaker Differentiation
- **Lt. Gen. Ted Martin:** Discussed doctrinal drafting, National Defense Strategy alignment, and TRADOC junior leader engagement via pre-recorded video.
- **Brig. Gen. Paul T. Stanton:** Spoke live at TechNet Augusta on commanders driving operations, demystifying the science, multi-domain data movement, and zero trust principles.

## Verbatim Attributed Quotations from Brig. Gen. Stanton
Stanton on commanders driving operations:
> "If there’s one theme across all this, it’s that we are driving operations for commanders. Commanders are the ones that make the decisions [and] commanders are the ones that develop the course of action and scheme of maneuver."

Stanton on commander comprehension of technical fundamentals:
> "We can help abstract away some of the complexity of the science, but our commanders have to understand the fundamentals because this is a huge part of how we’re going to fight in the future."

Stanton on joint data movement and information advantage:
> "I have to move the right data to the right place at the right time inside the decision cycle of the adversary, so that I can maintain [the] information advantage. I can’t do that without a unified network."

Stanton on protecting information and zero trust:
> "We’re pushing hard toward a zero-trust environment and an architecture to support and apply zero-trust principles across the entire Department of Defense Information Network."
