## Factual Summary
AFCEA reports on Army officials' remarks at TechNet Augusta, which it dates August 17–19, 2021. The article does not identify Stanton's precise speaking day or call his appearance a keynote; its format is therefore recorded as conference remarks, format unspecified. At the time Stanton was a Brigadier General and commander of the U.S. Army Cyber Center of Excellence. He discussed the commander's role in information advantage, understanding its scientific fundamentals, and the need for a unified network. The article separately attributes doctrine and future-force remarks to Lt. Gen. Ted Martin, who appeared by video.

## Short Attributed Excerpts from Brig. Gen. Stanton
On operations supporting commanders:
> “we are driving operations for commanders. Commanders are the ones that make the decisions.”

On understanding information advantage:
> “our commanders have to understand the fundamentals”

On network support to information advantage:
> “I have to move the right data to the right place at the right time”

These are verbatim excerpts attributed to Stanton by the article. Martin's comments and predictions about the waypoint force are not Stanton's remarks.
