## Factual Summary
SIGNAL Media covers presentations by Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton and Acting DoD CIO Katie Arrington at DISA's Forecast to Industry 2025 on December 8, 2025. Both leaders emphasized the imperative of rapidly delivering "functionally relevant capabilities" to combatant commands rather than delaying deployments while waiting for 100% perfect solutions. Stanton clarified that deploying an 80% solution does not mean settling for ineffective tools, but rather integrating existing commercial capabilities into an 80% system-of-systems that delivers immediate, meaningful operational value. Stanton also stressed that complex national security challenges cannot be solved by any single vendor, calling for agile contracting and deep multi-vendor integration.

## Short Attributed Style-Anchor Excerpts

### Functionally Relevant 80% Solutions
> “Eighty percent that isn't delivering something meaningful is the wrong 80%. You have to figure out, how do you put the pieces and parts together to create an 80% solution to a problem that is relevant for the users of the system? . . . The 80% is a system of systems that provides functionally relevant capability.”

### No Single Vendor Solves Interconnected Problems
> “There will be no individual company that solves all of our problems. All of our problems are interconnected.”

### Pace of Combatant Command Incorporation
> Stanton stated that the key to keeping up with adversaries is delivering “functionally relevant capabilities” at the same pace combatant commands can incorporate them into operations.
