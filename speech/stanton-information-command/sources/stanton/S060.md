## Factual Summary
SIGNAL Media reports on a media roundtable with Maj. Gen. Paul Stanton at the Association of the United States Army (AUSA) Annual Meeting in Washington, D.C. on October 12, 2023. Stanton discussed cyber force recruitment, operational modernization, and reducing technical complexity for combat soldiers. He revealed that candidate demand to join the Army Cyber Officer Corps stood at approximately 10 to 1, attracting high-performing college students with computer science and STEM backgrounds. Stanton detailed major institutional reforms, including consolidating the Signal Corps from 17 Military Occupational Specialties (MOSs) down to seven to ensure broad technical literacy across unified networks. He echoed Chief of Staff of the Army Gen. Randy George's mandate to simplify front-line user interfaces so systems function under severe weather and combat conditions, while ensuring specialized Signal and Cyber personnel manage the underlying network complexity. Stanton also addressed electronic warfare tactics, explaining that units must learn to "raise the noise floor" to obscure friendly operations and hide in plain sight.
