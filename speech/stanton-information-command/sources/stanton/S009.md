## Factual Summary
Reporting on Stanton's closing keynote on the last day of TechNet Augusta 2026. He argued that standardization and commonly understood data flows are needed to understand an operating environment and bound risk; he illustrated standardized interfaces with a Lego analogy. He said future NGC2 corps headquarters and infantry divisions should be able to “snap in” when moving to U.S. Pacific Command or U.S. Central Command, while explicitly noting that this does not happen now. The article discusses agentic capabilities in the operating environment but does not report a separate Stanton doctrine about synthetic data or model collapse.
