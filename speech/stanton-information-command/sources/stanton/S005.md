## Factual Summary
Reporting by George I. Seffers (with contribution from Nuray Taylor) on an exclusive interview with Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton following his assumption of leadership over DISA and JFHQ-DODIN in October 2024. Stanton laid out four top priorities for both organizations: readiness, campaigning, continuous modernization, and establishing lethality in cyberspace to deter adversary attacks. He emphasized that individual and collective readiness must be validated, that campaigning requires moving from a reactionary posture to proactive cost imposition in coordination with combatant commands, and that continuous modernization requires DISA to act as a combat support agency delivering integrated systems-of-systems.
