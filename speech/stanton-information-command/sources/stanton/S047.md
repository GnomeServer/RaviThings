## Factual Summary
Defense One reports on administrative and operational developments following the signing of Executive Order 14347 designating the Department of Defense as the Department of War. During keynote remarks at the Billington Cybersecurity Summit on September 11, 2025, Lt. Gen. Paul Stanton used the term "Department of War Information Network" while highlighting the Department's fundamental dependence on commercial industry technology. Stanton noted that defense networks are an amalgamation of commercial products assembled and adapted to meet warfighting requirements. He emphasized continuous modernization as his top-tier priority, declaring that the network cannot remain static. The article notes the legal and institutional nuances: while secondary titles are used in executive communications, statutorily the organization remains the Department of Defense under 10 U.S.C. § 111.

## Short Attributed Style-Anchor Excerpts

### Commercial Foundations of Defense Networks
> “When we talk about the Department of War Information Network, it doesn't exist without industry. It's nothing more than a nomination of industry products, and we put them together in a meaningful way. We make some minor adjustments and modifications to ensure that we meet the war fighting requirements.”

### Continuous Modernization Imperative
> “The DODIN today won't be the DODIN tomorrow, which is why my No. 2 priority is continuous modernization. We must keep pace with technology. We must keep pace with the adversary. We must keep pace with our mission command.”
