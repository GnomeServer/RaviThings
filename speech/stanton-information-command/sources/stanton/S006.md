## Factual Summary
Reporting on DISA's Forecast to Industry 2024, held October 28, 2024. The article reports DISA's FY2024 contract spending as $7.1 billion in a paragraph attributing related award/action counts and customer coverage to procurement services executive Douglas Packard; those figures are not Stanton's remarks. It separately reports CTO Steve Wallace's remarks on technology areas including AI. Stanton described the data goal as “the right data to the right place at the right time,” spoke about designing for uncertain joint-force coalitions, and named continuous modernization, simplicity and intuitiveness, and people who are “brilliant at the basics” as general design criteria for industry partners. The article does not attribute a claim about “data accuracy” to Stanton.
