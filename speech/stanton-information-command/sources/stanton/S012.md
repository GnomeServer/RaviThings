## Factual Summary
The article, datelined Towson, Maryland, reports Stanton's Monday keynote at DISA's annual Forecast to Industry event and a separate media roundtable. The visible article date is December 8, 2025, and that is the only supported date relationship for “Monday”; the previously circulated November 3 date is not supported by this article. Stanton described delivering functionally relevant capabilities as integrated systems solving a combatant commander's mission problem, rather than handing the commander a widget. At the separate roundtable, he said DISA was organizing existing work around combatant command requirements. Other quotations in the article come from DISA officials Jason Martin, Doug Packard, and Carlen Capenos.
