## Factual Summary
DefenseScoop interviewed Stanton at the AFCEA TechNet Cyber conference; the publication date does not establish the interview day. Stanton said Army Doctrinal Publication 3-13, Information Advantage, was on target for completion by the end of the summer. He described the Army's use of “information dimension” in contrast with joint doctrine's “information environment,” and discussed assessing theater information advantage detachments during Pacific Sentry. These are the article's 2023 reporting and not a claim about current doctrine.

## Short Attributed Excerpts from Maj. Gen. Stanton
On the Army's terminology:
> “We refer to the ‘information dimension’ of the operating environment and the joint world refers to the ‘information environment.’”

On the role of a theater information advantage detachment:
> “the theater information advantage detachment has the ability to deliver the effect”

Both are exact excerpts attributed to Stanton by the article. The article quotes Army Chief of Staff Gen. James McConville separately; his remarks are not attributed to Stanton here.
