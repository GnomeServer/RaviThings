## Multi-Speaker Attribution & Separation of Remarks
1. **Headline Attribution:** The headline's quoted phrase “Data is the new ammo” is attributed by the article to **Maj. Gen. Christopher Eubank**, commanding general of NETCOM, not Stanton. Eubank also said “there's a ton of it.”
2. **Other General Officers:** The article attributes unified-network remarks to **Lt. Gen. John Morrison** (Army G-6), and information-processing, AI/ML, and data-use remarks to **Lt. Gen. Maria Gervais** (TRADOC). Neither speaker's remarks are Stanton's.
3. **Industry Perspectives:** Article sections also cover views from Army Software Factory CISO Angelica Phaneuf, Southeastern Cyber CEO Christian Keebler, and CyberPoint CNO lead Alexander Levesque. Their remarks are not Stanton's.

## Verbatim Attributed Quotations & Role of Maj. Gen. Paul T. Stanton
At the time of TechNet Augusta 2022 (August 15–18, 2022), Stanton held the rank of **Major General**, serving as Commanding General of the U.S. Army Cyber Center of Excellence and Fort Gordon.

1. **Operationalizing the Signal Corps:**
   > “I’m your Huckleberry,” said Maj. Gen. Paul T. Stanton, commanding general of the Cyber Center of Excellence and Fort Gordon.
   *(The article says Army organizational networks had come under Cyber Command's operational control and Stanton would help lead the Signal Corps' shift from mission support to embedded operations.)*

2. **Electromagnetic Spectrum & Emissions Control:**
   > “But the principles and the foundation won’t,” he said.
   *(The article reports Stanton related lessons from Ukraine to digital emissions control and understanding the electromagnetic footprint; in the quoted passage he said available EW tools change but principles and foundations do not.)*

3. **Zero Trust Curriculum Development:**
   > “We absolutely need to start today in building the bench for our future,” he said.
   *(The article reports Stanton said the Cyber Center of Excellence was developing Zero Trust instruction and pushing completed content to active-area warrant officers.)*
