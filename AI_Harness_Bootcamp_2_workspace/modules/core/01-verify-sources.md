**Serves oracle:** S04, S07, S08, S09, S14, S19  
**Primary objective:** PO-01 — Verify sources and outputs  
**Prerequisites:** Preflighted accessible environment and this module's supplied case  
**Consumes:** VERIFY:PREFLIGHT; VERIFY:CASE; VERIFY:SOURCE_FIXTURES  
**Produces:** SOURCE_EVIDENCE; DISCERNMENT_RESULT; STANDING_RULE; PO01_RESULT  
**Rough time:** about 3 hours  
**Performance stage:** Independent  
**Work surface:** Research/source work  
**Practical work:** Start a work folder with the supplied starter. Hash the inbox (nine sources). Freeze identity in the source register. Build the eight-step thread ledger and recompute the six required calculations from the sources. Write the challenge matrix rejecting misleading and hostile sources. Run the producer rebuttal with --fixture (prints PRACTICE warning and provenance; live path uses the shared launcher and write-only producer-rebuttal.md; a failed child is never success). Write the corrected brief and render the review surface. Check that the surface alone answers the five questions. Freeze baseline verdict and prediction. Reveal the sealed change. Update only dependent claims in the changed ledger, brief, and verdict. The stretch traces every material changed/unchanged claim after the sealed update and defends one unchanged claim with a counterfactual. All core actions use explicit workdir and absolute source helper paths in paired Bash/PowerShell examples with Expected, Stop, and Recovery after each group. 
**Performance evidence:** Independent known-answer result, SOURCE_EVIDENCE, DISCERNMENT_RESULT, misleading/irrelevant-source result, changed-source delta, STANDING_RULE, and internal verdict. Frozen reference and source hashes remain untouched. Nine baseline sources, eight rows, six calculations, and one sealed update are preserved exactly. 
**Handoff:** Give the next owner the source boundary, the traced claim, the checks that decided it, the standing rule, and the verdict.
**Case family:** [CASE_FAMILY.md](../../CASE_FAMILY.md). The adapter supplies a self-contained case in that family. This module’s gate does not consume another module’s product.


## Why

Fluent output can conceal an unsupported claim, an irrelevant source, or a changed fact. Verification is an independently evidenced action, not another request to the producer for reassurance.

## Enabling objectives

1. Distinguish source fact, model transformation, inference, and human decision.
2. Trace one consequential claim from the artifact to its source of record.
3. Predict changed-source behavior while proving misleading material stays outside the result.

## Check the work

Compare the known answer with the source, trace the interpretation to supporting text, exclude misleading material, and compare the changed-source result with the frozen prediction. Record the independent internal accept, revise, reject, or hold decision in PO01_RESULT. A material unsupported claim blocks acceptance of the brief even when the rest looks correct.
