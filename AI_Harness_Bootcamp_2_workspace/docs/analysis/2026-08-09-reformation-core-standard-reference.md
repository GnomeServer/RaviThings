# Standard Reference: Reformation Core Professional AI-Harness Course

Date frozen: 2026-08-09  
Mode: Gauntlet  
Tier: Standard  
Scope: Core course structure, learning objectives, sequence, and evidence contracts only  
Status: **READ-ONLY AFTER HASHING**

## Assumptions

1. The learner is a professional domain user with ordinary workplace digital competence, not necessarily a developer.
2. The learner will use contemporary generative-AI products that may expose files, grounding sources, reusable instructions, tools, and managed workflows.
3. The common core qualifies professional AI use and bounded harness operation. It does not qualify model development, API/MCP implementation, retrieval-pipeline construction, agent-runtime engineering, or deployment.
4. The course is facilitated and performance-based. Self-study reading may support it but cannot substitute for observed gates.
5. The design case is 20 learners; the 10x case is 200 learners.
6. The target seat-time is 30 facilitated hours **including** 20 hours of supervised or independently evidenced practice. These are design bounds, not measured delivery facts.
7. Product adapters and fixtures will be built later. This Reference judges whether the skeleton gives those implementations an internally complete and bounded contract.
8. Provider curricula establish field coverage and role boundaries, not comparative educational effectiveness.
9. Current legal and policy sources are a 2026-08-09 snapshot. The course must require contextual policy decisions without claiming universal compliance.

# Part 1 — The Reference

## 1. The need

Professional domain users, when they move from occasional AI assistance to recurring work that informs a real decision, need to select appropriate work, direct it, verify it, control its data and authority, distinguish ordinary model variation from material change, recover safely, and transfer the method—preventing polished unsupported output, unsafe release, brittle chat-only processes, and hidden builder responsibilities.

The stronger framing is not “teach an AI harness.” It is **qualify a professional to produce accountable AI-assisted work at the smallest sufficient operating complexity**.

**Done:** on an unfamiliar low-risk task, a nondeveloper independently produces a useful artifact, verifies its decisive claims, applies a contextual responsibility screen before any release, operates one bounded reusable method, handles a material adverse condition without weakening acceptance, distinguishes a real method change from ordinary variation, and leaves a package that passes both clean-session and independent-person use—while escalating advanced agent-system needs instead of implementing them.

### Survey result

No exemplar spans the whole need. The ideal is a composite:

- Anthropic-style delegation, description, discernment, and diligence;
- Google/Microsoft-style workplace artifacts and workflows;
- low-code-agent curricula as the boundary marker, not the universal core;
- NIST/EU/DigComp/UNESCO for contextual responsibility and progression;
- NICE/FAA/LTEM for task–knowledge–risk–skill traceability and transfer.

## 3. Governing constraints and invariants

### Non-negotiable invariants

1. **Professional artifact first:** the course produces a checked useful result before architecture depth.
2. **Responsibility before release:** no learner can claim `RELEASE` before applying at least a minimum contextual screen for data rights, affected people, disclosure, authority, and decision ownership.
3. **Human decision ownership:** AI cannot silently own acceptance, release, refusal, authority expansion, or residual-risk acceptance.
4. **Independent evidence:** every material acceptance uses an observation outside the producing path or protected from it.
5. **Source integrity:** citations, known answers, and decision-relevant claims are inspected at the source of record where possible.
6. **Variation is observable:** stochastic model output is not mistaken for method change or exact workflow delta. Deterministic outer state and probabilistic content are scored separately.
7. **Least-powerful sufficient method:** one interaction precedes reusable instructions; a fixed workflow precedes persistent state, adaptive flow, or multiple workers.
8. **Nondeveloper core:** the learner specifies and configures bounded behavior in supplied controls; implementation and protected custody remain owned by an adapter or separate builder role.
9. **Correct refusal can pass:** when a tool or release is unwarranted, a supported refusal satisfies the decision branch; operation evidence is required only when the mechanism is selected.
10. **Prerequisite sufficiency:** every prerequisite is produced and evidenced by an earlier gate or explicitly verified at the start of the consuming module.
11. **No partial-credit laundering:** bounded evidence and `HOLD` may permit continued learning but cannot satisfy a separately owned program outcome unless that outcome is reassessed against its original gate.
12. **Failure preserves standards:** repair follows sealed diagnosis, does not weaken acceptance, and is checked under the original condition.
13. **Adversarial composition:** untrusted content and bounded tool authority are tested together, not only in isolation.
14. **Separate transfer claims:** clean-session restartability cannot substitute for independent-person use.
15. **Vendor neutrality:** competencies survive replacement of product, model, or scenario.
16. **Accessibility:** equivalent paths must expose the same raw state and permit the same consequential learner action; narration is not equivalent operation.
17. **Simulation honesty:** simulated or discussed behavior cannot be reported as executed performance.
18. **Skeleton boundary:** core files define outcomes, ownership, sequence, prerequisites, practical work classes, evidence, failure, scope, handoff, and budgets—not detailed lesson or adapter procedures.

### Legal and policy constraints

- The core must require context-specific policy, rights, privacy, disclosure, and oversight decisions; it cannot certify compliance across jurisdictions or sectors.
- EU Article 4 guidance is role-, knowledge-, context-, purpose-, and risk-sensitive. A generic certificate is insufficient evidence for every deployment context.
- NIST and OECD-style trustworthiness principles are guidance references unless separately made binding by policy or law.
- Copyright, privacy, accessibility, employment, records, safety, and sector obligations require named local owners and escalation.

### Economic and ergonomic constraints

- The core contains at most **10 modules**.
- Facilitated seat-time is **24–35 hours**, including practice.
- Practice occupies at least **60%** of facilitated time.
- The first checked useful artifact appears within **60 minutes** in a preflighted environment.
- Each module has exactly **one primary outcome** and no more than **three enabling objectives**.
- Each module leaves one evidence bundle; no second full record system is mandatory.
- Core completion requires **zero programming objectives**.
- Authoritative module skeletons total at most **900 lines**; each is at most **110 lines**.
- **Provisional delivery target:** external variable model/tool spend ≤ **US$40 per learner**. This cannot be reported achieved until timed usage telemetry includes retries and make-up attempts.
- **Provisional scoring target:** median human scoring ≤ **15 minutes per learner per gate**, 90th percentile ≤ **25 minutes**. This cannot be reported achieved until timed judges include moderation and `HOLD` review.
- **Provisional inter-rater target:** ≥ **90% exact agreement** on binary hard gates and weighted κ ≥ **0.70** on ordinal judgment fields, measured on at least 20 double-scored performances. These are design guesses pending pilot evidence.

### Tradeoff frontier

| Axis | Ideal position | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Broad AI literacy ↔ harness depth | Three common work surfaces plus one fixed reusable method | Gives workplace relevance without forcing agent engineering. |
| Early utility ↔ safety | Checked artifact first; minimum responsibility screen before any release; full tool/release gate later | Avoids lecture-first delay without allowing unsafe early release. |
| User judgment ↔ implementation | Learner specifies, configures, tests, and decides; adapter implements and protects | Preserves causal control without hidden builder work. |
| Determinism ↔ probabilistic value | Deterministic outer gates plus explicit repeated controls for material stochastic output | Exact operational claims remain defensible. |
| Authenticity ↔ comparability | Replaceable domain scenarios under stable artifact and evidence contracts | Real work remains relevant while gates remain comparable. |
| Completeness ↔ parsimony | One owner per sub-problem, at most ten modules | Avoids duplicate cycles and orphaned prerequisites. |
| Continue learning ↔ qualification integrity | `HOLD` may permit participation; final qualification requires every program outcome | Prevents one missing competency from disappearing inside capstone completion. |
| Scale ↔ judgment quality | Mechanically protected facts plus calibrated narrow human rubrics | Cohort growth cannot turn performance assessment into file checking. |

## 4. The ideal, characterized

### Normal conditions

A learner enters a preflighted workspace, sees the intended work product and decision, and produces a checked result within 60 minutes. Before the artifact leaves the bounded learning environment, the learner applies a minimum responsibility screen. The sequence then develops independent direction, source verification, context/control placement, full responsible release and tool-authority judgment, failure recovery, observed-run improvement, one fixed workflow, controlled change evaluation with variation treatment, and transfer.

Each module has one owned performance claim. Its predecessor provides the exact evidence needed for its first action. The learner may reuse an earlier skill, but the curriculum does not redefine it.

### Edge conditions

- A learner whose real task is too sensitive, consequential, sparse, or unsuitable uses an authentic bounded case; workplace-transfer claims remain unproved.
- A supported no-use, no-release, or no-tool decision can pass the decision objective without fabricated operation.
- A repair outside learner authority yields localization evidence and `HOLD`, not full recovery credit.
- Fewer than the required observed runs yields a smaller evidence exercise or `HOLD`; fabricated volume is prohibited.
- A task that truly needs persistent state, adaptive flow, or several workers ends in a supported escalation or moves to an advanced qualification.
- Equivalent accessible controls can replace an inaccessible native surface only when they expose the same raw state and causal action.

### Adversarial conditions

The core includes protected conditions for:

- a polished unsupported claim;
- a misleading or irrelevant source;
- instruction-like untrusted content;
- a malformed or missing required input;
- producer check-tampering;
- excessive or omitted authority;
- untrusted content attempting to cause a tool action;
- a rule change with unexpected blast radius;
- an instructor-controlled hidden fault;
- a candidate change whose apparent gain is ordinary run-to-run variation;
- an unresolved prior `HOLD` presented as course completion.

The learner must reject, contain, hold, recover, or escalate without weakening the oracle.

### Degraded conditions

Missing model, source, tool, protected fixture, recipient, accessible view, or restore path produces one of three explicit states:

1. equivalent same-state execution;
2. bounded evidence for an unaffected objective; or
3. `HOLD` for the affected objective.

No discussion, replay, or paper trace becomes executed evidence. Resumption starts from the last verified state and preserves the original gate.

### 10x scale

At 200 learners, program outcomes, prerequisite proofs, protected cases, rubrics, qualification rules, and transfer claims remain unchanged. Automation may scale mechanical checks. Human judges still score contextual responsibility, ambiguous evidence, recovery reasoning, and transfer. Hidden-case custody, scorer calibration, double scoring, moderation, appeals, and independent-recipient capacity receive explicit staffing and time accounting.

A scale plan fails if it samples only some learners, replaces person transfer with a model session, reveals hidden cases through reuse, or exceeds declared budgets without a `HOLD` or revised delivery claim.

### Encountering the ideal

In the first ten seconds, the learner sees the professional artifact, intended decision, source boundary, and first safe action. They do not see installation, architecture taxonomy, evidence jargon, or a compliance lecture.

After completion, the learner no longer relies on conversational memory, model confidence, prompt folklore, broad credentials, a single lucky run, or live author coaching. The saved direction, source evidence, controls, variation record, failure boundary, decision, and handoff carry those burdens.

## 5. The off-axis frontier

| Rank | Approach | Mechanism | Buys | Costs | Right answer when |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Common operator core plus role qualifications | Aviation type-rating model: shared user/operator core; separate persistent-state, adaptive, multi-agent, and builder ratings. | Clear occupational boundary and preserved advanced depth. | More explicit credentials and routing. | Cohort roles differ in the machinery they will operate. |
| **2** | Integrated scenario spiral | One professional case accumulates direction, verification, controls, tools, workflow, change, recovery, and transfer. | Low orientation burden and visible cumulative value. | Scenario blind spots and weak breadth if not counterbalanced. | Cohort shares a domain or work pattern. |
| **3** | Competency stations | Healthcare OSCE/FAA-style short stations independently test delegation, verification, authority, diagnosis, variation, and handoff. | Comparable performance evidence and test-out support. | Fragmentation, examiner effort, station gaming. | Certification consistency dominates sustained project authenticity. |
| **4** | Workplace apprenticeship | Real workflow improved through coached cycles and observed on-job transfer. | Highest ecological validity and real outcome evidence. | Privacy, instructor load, inconsistent cases, delayed evidence. | Small trusted cohorts have mature real work. |
| **5** | Managed approved assistants — dissolve the problem | Organization supplies locked role-specific assistants and workflows; users only select tasks, inspect output, disclose limits, and escalate. | Removes most local harness design, authority, and recovery burden. | Low portability and limited adaptation/diagnosis. | Central governance intentionally owns all runtime behavior. |
| **6** | Governance license | Risk classification, policy, records, and approval precede any practical production. | Rapid assurance coverage and clear accountability. | Can produce policy fluency without operating competence or early value. | Role is audit, procurement, oversight, or deployment approval. |
| **7** | Self-paced portfolio certificate | Learner completes artifact projects and reflective evidence asynchronously. | Lower delivery cost and broad access. | Weak identity verification, adversarial control, transfer, and scorer calibration. | Goal is exposure and portfolio formation, not qualification. |

Ranking defense: the common-core/type-rating model preserves practical AI use and harness judgment without disguising agent engineering as universal literacy. The scenario spiral is the strongest delivery pattern but cannot alone define occupational boundaries. Stations strengthen certification; apprenticeship strengthens workplace transfer. Managed assistants become preferable when the organization can remove local harness decisions entirely.

### Named semantic judges

**Curriculum judge:** an experienced workforce educator or AI-enablement lead who did not author the skeleton. Score each semantic row `0 absent / 1 present but vague or internally inconsistent / 2 observable, bounded, and aligned`. Every applicable row must score 2 for PASS.

**Scope judge:** lists every required learner action and assigns `professional user / harness operator / low-code builder / software engineer`. PASS requires zero builder/engineer actions in common completion and explicit ownership for adapter implementation.

**Prerequisite seam judge:** for each module, maps every prerequisite noun to a prior evidence field or consuming-module verification. Any orphan or circular dependency is FAIL.

**Evaluation judge:** checks whether deterministic and stochastic claims are separated and whether repeated-control rules are sufficient for the named decision. A single unqualified model sample supporting method superiority is FAIL.

**Responsible-use judge:** applies a composed case involving rights, affected people, disclosure, and tool authority. PASS requires interaction-aware decision evidence, not six isolated boxes.

**Recipient judge:** an independent person performs an evaluator-selected task. Protected rubric fields: purpose/bounds, operation, evidence interpretation, responsibility/limits, stop/restore, and next-owner handoff. All six pass on the preserved first attempt.

**Scale/degradation judge:** traces 20- and 200-learner cohorts and missing-dependency cases. Policy preservation can pass from the skeleton; delivery feasibility stays provisional without staffing and telemetry.

### Absolute failure conditions

Any observation below makes the implementation not ideal even if all other tests pass:

1. A learner claims consequential `RELEASE` before a minimum responsibility screen.
2. Correct no-use/no-tool refusal fails solely because no operation occurred.
3. A module consumes prerequisite evidence that no earlier module produces or verifies.
4. Localization-only is reported as completion of recovery.
5. Learner completion requires implementing a dynamic checker, API, MCP server, RAG pipeline, agent runtime, or deployment.
6. A single stochastic output supports an unqualified exact-delta or candidate-superiority claim.
7. Untrusted content and tool authority are never tested together.
8. A capstone pass erases an unresolved program outcome.
9. A material artifact passes using only producer self-assessment.
10. Simulation or replay is reported as executed performance.
11. Clean-session operation substitutes for independent-person transfer.
12. Core exceeds ten modules, 35 facilitated hours, or zero-code boundary.
13. A declared budget is reported measured without telemetry.
14. The Standard Reference changes after test execution begins.

## 7. Anti-goals

Excellence does not include:

- product tours, exhaustive feature coverage, or one module per workplace application;
- model training, fine-tuning, ML theory, benchmark engineering, API/MCP construction, custom RAG, agent-runtime development, or deployment;
- mandatory persistent-state, adaptive-loop, or multi-agent operation;
- detailed lesson scripts, adapter commands, forms, answer keys, or instructor fixtures in the stable core;
- legal-compliance certification;
- generic ethics recall detached from release behavior;
- statistical calibration claims from a bootcamp-sized forecast series;
- stable mastery claims from a few hidden faults;
- a requirement to automate arbitrary semantic failures;
- exact-output claims where material model variation is uncontrolled;
- a capstone that compensates for missing component outcomes;
- multiple evidence ladders, operating cycles, or full record systems;
- precise delivery-performance claims before pilot telemetry;
- preserving already-written material when it does not serve an oracle criterion.

## 8. Obsolescence

This Reference becomes wrong within eighteen months if:

1. managed workplace systems reliably expose protected checks, source provenance, permissions, variation controls, rollback, and transfer receipts as default inspectable behavior;
2. bounded persistent-state or agent operation becomes a universal professional baseline supported by managed controls rather than local system design;
3. role-specific regulation mandates materially different literacy or oversight outcomes;
4. evidence shows fixed workflow is no longer the smallest practical reusable rung for contemporary AI work;
5. common products remove stochastic variation from the relevant professional tasks or expose robust uncertainty controls that make repeated-run treatment obsolete;
6. pilots show nondevelopers cannot complete the objective set within 35 hours using accessible supplied mechanics;
7. employers cease to value transferable method evidence because approved managed assistants dissolve local operation.

Earliest signals:

- major office suites ship standardized evidence, authorization, evaluation, and rollback receipts;
- nontechnical foundation certificates require and reliably assess persistent or multi-agent operation;
- official competency frameworks publish role-specific AI-operator proficiency standards;
- two pilots miss the first-value, practice, scorer-time, or qualification targets by more than 20%;
- scope judges repeatedly classify saved guard/check/workflow actions as builder work;
- inter-rater agreement remains below the provisional thresholds after rubric revision.

Review the signals quarterly. Create a successor Reference when one fires; do not rewrite this frozen Standard during the Gauntlet.
