# Request · North Shelf status brief

Write a status brief from the Harbor Depot inventory clerk to the Field Clinic S-3 supply team about the North Shelf request.

The brief must:

- start with a title line;
- have 5 or 6 sections, each with a numbered heading, and 600 to 900 words in all;
- use only the six files in `sources/`;
- answer the five questions in `S6-clinic-message.md` with what the sources support, and say plainly when the sources don't give an answer;
- say what was requested, what is on hand, what a release needs, who owns any release, what is unknown, and what the clinic must not do yet;
- end with the contact for paperwork questions.

The brief must not become a release, a vehicle assignment, a permit, a receipt, or a dispatch. It's for the Field Clinic S-3 supply team only, not for patients, a public notice, or an operations list.
