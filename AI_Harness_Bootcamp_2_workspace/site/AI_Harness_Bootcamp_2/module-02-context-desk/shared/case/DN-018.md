# Carbon clip where received and released collide

**Note:** DN-018
**Issuer:** Quarry Depot dock
**Date:** 7 October 2026
**Document type:** stamp clip
**Crate:** C-44

The left carbon on crate C-44's clip is stamped received at 10:05 MDT at the Quarry Depot dock. The right carbon was a misfile for crate C-45. That misfile is stamped released and then crossed out. The crossed-out word does not apply to C-44.

Received and released are different condition words. On this clip the applicable word for crate C-44 is received. Reading the crossed-out released line as the C-44 status would turn custody into a release the quality office did not issue.
