# Three different endings for the same crate

**Note:** DN-038
**Issuer:** Quarry Depot desk
**Date:** 7 October 2026
**Document type:** desk distinction
**Crate:** C-44
**Vehicle:** QP-17

Destination custody would mean Clinic P-4 had received crate C-44. A quality release would mean the quality office had signed the instrument sets for use. A clinic-confirmed usable effect would mean the clinic had recorded that those released sets were usable. None of those three endings is in this pile.

The clerk's stamp would skip all three and commit the crate to vehicle QP-17. This note names the gap. It does not fill it, and it does not state an inner height.
