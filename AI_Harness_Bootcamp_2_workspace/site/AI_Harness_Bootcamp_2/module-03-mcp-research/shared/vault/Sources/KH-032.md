# KH-032 · Heavy vehicle advisory

Advisory for heavy vehicle drivers.

One bridge on MSR Heron has a reduced posted class after the 7 October inspection. Loaded trucks of class 26 must not cross it. Drivers should check the bridge list before movement and ask the engineer cell about exceptions. A second bridge, on an alternate route, is closed until 100200Z OCT 2026.
