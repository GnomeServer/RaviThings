# KH-036 · Tanner Yard transload hours

Tanner Yard can transfer cargo between vehicles from 0600L to 1800L. Lifting equipment limits the yard to one vehicle at a time. Cold-chain cargo is moved first.

The yard does not store medical stock. Requests for yard time go to the yard supervisor at least 12 hours ahead. A vehicle that arrives without a request waits until the yard is clear.
