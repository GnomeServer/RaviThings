# KH-001 · Storage guidance for burn-dressing cases

Burn-dressing cases should be stored dry, between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius, away from direct sun. Cases must stay in their sealed outer carton until issue. Do not stack more than five cartons high.

Cases that have been wet or frozen must be reported to the QA office before use. Expired cases are quarantined and returned to the supply point.
