# KH-014 · Route status: MSR Heron

Route clearance status as of 081600Z OCT 2026.

MSR Heron is open to all traffic between Mill Depot and FB Brandt. Surface condition is good. Posted speed is 40 km/h through the wadi crossing and 60 km/h elsewhere. The route is open to daylight and night movement, subject to the curfew at the FB Brandt gates.

ASR Linden is a separate alternate route and is not covered by this report. ASR Larch leads to FB Brenner and is not a route to this base.
