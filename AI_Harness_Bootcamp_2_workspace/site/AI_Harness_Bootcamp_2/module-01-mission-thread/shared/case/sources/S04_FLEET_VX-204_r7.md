## Mission payload

The maximum mission payload for **VX-204** is **1,650 kg**. Mission payload includes cargo, removable racks, and other removable equipment carried for the movement.

Cold Lantern medicine kits require refrigeration rack **RR-84**. The rack weighs **84 kg** and must be included in the payload calculation.

## Similar vehicle warning

Vehicle **VX-240** uses a different body and rack configuration. Documents for VX-240 do not establish payload or equipment requirements for VX-204.
