Put an AI harness to work

An AI harness is the working environment around a model: its instructions, source files, tools, permissions, and records of what happened. You control that environment so the model can do a defined job and you can check the result.

You'll use Oh My Pi to work with files, supplied tools, and small agent teams; Obsidian to review and link notes; and a local copy of n8n to build visual workflows in Module 7. You'll save instructions, delegate independent assignments, recover blocked work, and decide whether an output holds up against its sources. You don't need any programming experience: you'll paste supplied commands, edit instructions and settings, and open the files yourself to see what they contain.

Produce work you can check

  • A long document you can trust. Plan a status brief before any prose, have the model draft it one section at a time, check it in separate review sessions, and revise only what's wrong.
  • A defensible brief. Trace claims to the right sources, reproduce calculations, and separate supported facts from inference and unresolved questions.
  • A reusable knowledge vault. Review source-backed notes in Obsidian, link the useful claims, and prove that a fresh model session uses your saved instruction and only the notes you approved. Then fix one weakness that matters and show the difference in another fresh run.
  • A checked agent-team brief. Give OMP specialists separate assignments, inspect their sources and results, and combine only accepted work. If one agent is blocked, preserve the valid reports, repair that assignment, and recheck the combined brief.
  • A spreadsheet an agent writes. In local n8n, connect an AI Agent to OpenRouter with your key and have it write a spreadsheet from a batch. Download the file and check it against the source. The chat is not the result.
  • A corrected, evidence-bound brief. Check each material claim, have independent agents challenge it, and verify the corrections against the original sources. Keep unknowns visible even when the reviewers agree.
  • A local model you can run yourself. Start it on your laptop, stop it, bring it back, and keep the files so you can do that again without the chat.

The spreadsheet is the result. A fluent reply in the agent panel is not the file, and a note inside the batch is not a rule.

An answer can read well and still be wrong, so compare what the tool actually produced with the request and the sources. If a check fails, save the failed result, fix the cause, and check again. If you're missing a fact or permission you need, stop and say exactly what's missing.

Work through the assignments

Start with setup, then work through the assignments in order. Each one comes with its own case and files and expects you to bring the skills you practiced in the earlier ones. Keep your work and evidence outside your checkout, the local copy of the course repository.

  1. 00North ShelfSet up the harness and get a long document you can trustGet Oh My Pi to plan, draft, check, and revise a long status brief, one section at a time, and decide whether you'd send it.Get an AI tool to write a multi-section document from sources that stays true to them, and decide from evidence whether you'd send it.
  2. 01Cold LanternVerify sources and outputsCheck a polished brief against the right sources and your own calculations.Test a polished AI recommendation against its sources and your own arithmetic, and give a supported accept, revise, reject, or hold verdict.
  3. 02Ledger PikeBuild and control a reusable second brainTurn AI judgments into linked notes under a saved rule, check the answer-to-source trail, and use feedback to improve a new revision without overwriting the first.Direct AI judgments into reusable linked notes under a saved rule, check their source support, and use feedback to improve a new revision while preserving the first.
  4. 03Kiln HoldResearch the notes, then limit what the AI can touchResearch a folder of notes with the assistant, check its markings against the rules, and show the limits you set.Connect the assistant to a folder of notes only after you read what that connection claims, limit it to the folders and actions each part of the work needs, prove those limits, and remove the connection with proof that it is gone.
  5. 04Chalk LineDecide with typed questionsTake one shift's messages, turn them into answers a script can sort, and check those answers against your own reading before you trust them.Ask one thing at a time, with answers a script can sort, add one question of your own, run the model once so it only answers, check those answers against labels you wrote first, and set the gates so the script and a named person decide what happens to each message.
  6. 05Copper SpanOrchestrate an OMP agent teamDelegate independent work, check each handoff, and recover a blocked agent before combining the results.Direct an OMP agent team whose assignments fit together, check what each agent actually did, and recover a partial failure without throwing away valid work.
  7. 06Blue GaugeUse Jev inside Oh My PiUse Jev inside Oh My Pi to work a military airlift desk: ask questions together, defer uncertain messages, prioritize competing support needs, and select the right handler. Compare actual calls, routes, rankings, and results.Use Jev inside Oh My Pi to configure and compare four decision-model patterns, choosing how answers become routes, priorities, and calls to the right kind of help.
  8. 07White RackAutomate a spreadsheet with an agentBuild one n8n workflow that asks a model for a spreadsheet and writes the file.Build one local n8n workflow, connect it to OpenRouter with your own key, and download the spreadsheet that workflow writes from a batch you give it.
  9. 08Slope BriefControl hallucinationsCheck a brief that sounds ready. Two reviewers can agree and still be wrong.Check a brief claim by claim, keep a correction from grading itself, and decide what the sources support.
  10. 09Night DeskConstrain agent behaviorRun a declared tool policy and inspect what the agent actually attempted.Show, from records rather than the agent's own words, what one AI agent actually tried to do and what stopped it.
  11. 10Cold FoundryStand up and package a local uncensored AIRun an uncensored model on your own laptop, stop it, bring it back, and keep the files so you can do that again.Run an uncensored model on your own laptop, stop it, bring it back, and keep the files so you can do that again without the chat.
