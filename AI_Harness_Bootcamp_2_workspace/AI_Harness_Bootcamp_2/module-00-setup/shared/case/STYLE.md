# Style rules

1. Say the fact plainly. Cut stock phrases such as "please be advised", "at this time", and "rest assured".
2. Cut explanation the reader doesn't need to act correctly.
3. Don't hedge a fact the sources state plainly, and don't make a fact sound more certain than its source.
4. Don't repeat a point another section already makes, unless the section's job is to sum up.
5. Prefer short sentences and common words.
6. Keep numbers, names, and times exactly as the sources give them.
