## Review standard

The module must go deep rather than broad. The end-to-end claim must open into smaller claims about identity, authority, time, quantity, condition, dependency, handoff, and uncertainty. Each smaller claim must terminate in a direct source observation, a deterministic calculation, a named assumption, an unresolved item, or a human decision.

A locally true statement cannot rescue a broken handoff. Receipt is not release. Release is not authorization. Arrival is not delivery. Delivery is not usable effect.

Score each category 0–4:

1. source and mission-thread correctness;
2. recursive depth without domain sprawl;
3. claim/source/authority precision;
4. arithmetic and time correctness;
5. misleading-source and hostile-content resistance;
6. changed-source dependency reasoning;
7. functional decision-surface evidence;
8. assessment integrity and `HOLD` behavior;
9. accessibility, facilitation, and transfer;
10. parsimony and learner voice.

Acceptance requires **40/40**, no blocker, no unsupported execution claim, and no absolute failure from Reference §11.

## Voice bar

The learner-facing prose must score:

- **Human craft authority: 100/100**
- **Detectable AI mannerisms: 0/100**

These are editorial panel scores, not scientific detector measurements. Any lower result must cite exact sentences. Reject generic framing, institutional evaluation jargon, fake warmth, marketing cadence, fragment chains, repeated summaries, abstract nouns where a concrete action exists, and prose that sounds written for a rubric instead of a person doing the work.
