## m08-claim-check

- Title: Find the value. Then read the whole record.
- A claim and its cited record. Code splits: value not in the record is fabricated with no call; value found goes to jev-1.13 with the whole record. Three outcomes: supports, contradicts, says nothing. A confidence below the gate waits for the person; moving the gate reads saved answers.

## m08-verify-escalate

- Title: Ask how likely each claim is wrong.
- A claim and its packet go to one call with four yes-means-wrong questions: value absent, different vehicle/clinic/shipment, dropped unit/zone/qualification, permission no record grants. Code keeps the highest. At or above the gate the claim escalates to a stronger model or the person for a proposal; below it the claim keeps the cheap verdict and is unflagged, not supported.

## m08-answer-in-packet

- Title: Does the packet hold the answer?
- The question a claim answers and every packet record as options. A choice always names a record; a separate yes-or-no answer says whether any record answers and can be near zero. A named record with a low yes-or-no means no source and a null value.

## m08-fan-out

- Title: Ask twice. Do not share the answer.
- Claims and sources split to a source reviewer and a skeptical reviewer. Each has its own session. The other review, the notes, and the exact findings are not sent.

## m08-one-writer

- Title: One agent writes the file.
- Source review, skeptical review, and exact checks feed one correcting agent. A review is not an edit. null means unknown. Agreement does not choose the value.

## m08-fresh-review

- Title: Check the rewrite without the old answers.
- Corrected claims and original sources go to two new sessions. First verdicts and writer notes are not sent.
