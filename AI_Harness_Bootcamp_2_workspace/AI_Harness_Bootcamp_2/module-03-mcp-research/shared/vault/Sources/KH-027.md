# KH-027 · Cold-chain checklist for receiving clerks

Checklist for the clinic receiving clerk.

1. Check the outer carton for intact seals.
2. Check storage conditions on arrival: dry, between 15 and 25 degrees Celsius.
3. Record the lot number and expiry date.
4. Report wet or frozen cartons to the QA office.
5. Stack no more than five cartons high.

This checklist restates the BMLO storage guidance in [[KH-001]].
