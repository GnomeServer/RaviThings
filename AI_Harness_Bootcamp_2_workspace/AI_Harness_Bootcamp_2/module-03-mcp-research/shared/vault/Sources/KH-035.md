# KH-035 · Partner liaison access schedule

Partner liaison staff may visit Clinic B-2 between 0900L and 1100L on weekdays, escorted by clinic staff. Visits outside those hours need approval from the clinic commander.

Visitors must not photograph the supply room. Requests for access are made through the liaison desk at least one day ahead.
