# KH-013 · Main gate hours and visitor access

The main gate is staffed from 0400L to 2300L. Between 2300L and 0400L only the north gate is open, and only for emergency traffic.

Visitor badges are issued at the liaison desk from 0800L to 1700L. Vehicles are searched on entry. Visitors must carry photo identification and must be met by their host at the gate.
