# KH-022 · Clinic B-2 receiving capacity

Clinic B-2 can receive one vehicle at its dock at a time and needs 45 minutes to count in a delivery of 40 cases. The dock is staffed from 0600L to 2000L. After hours, deliveries wait at the base gate until the dock opens.

Approved by the BMLO for sharing with the PMLC.
