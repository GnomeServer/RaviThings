# KH-006 · Movement curfew at base gates

All vehicle movement through the FB Brandt gates is restricted from 2200L to 0500L daily. Local time is UTC plus three hours.

Exceptions need approval from the base duty officer. Medical emergency traffic is exempt and should call ahead to the gate. This notice applies to every unit on the base, including visiting teams and contractors.
