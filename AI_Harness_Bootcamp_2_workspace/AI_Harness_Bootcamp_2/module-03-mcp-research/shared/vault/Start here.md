# Start here

You are a staff action officer in Task Force Marlin at Forward Base Brandt, working for the Base Medical Logistics Officer (BMLO). The task force's burn-care clinic, Clinic B-2, is running low on burn-dressing cases, and the BMLO has asked you for two products.

**Question.** What is known about getting 40 burn-dressing cases from Mill Depot to Clinic B-2 by 100600Z OCT 2026, what blocks it, and what is still unknown?

**Products.**

1. Fact notes and an open-questions note that answer the question, with every claim linked to the notes that support it (in `Drafts/research/`).
2. A short extract for the partner medical liaison cell, in `Drafts/partner/`. The extract may draw only on notes that are cleared for the partner audience.

**Where things live.**

- `Sources/` holds the forty notes in the research pile, KH-001 to KH-040.
- `Handbook/` holds the handling rules, the role of the Release Authority, and a glossary of report terms.
- `Drafts/` is where the AI writes: `Drafts/research/` for the research products and `Drafts/partner/` for the partner extract.
- `Estimate/` is your own work: your calibration, your handling register, and the folder of notes you release to the partner phase.

**Handling.** Every note is handled by the rules in [[Handling rules]]. The Release Authority is described in [[Release authority]]. A note's handling is a decision you make from the rules, not a label you copy.

**Calibration notes.** Before the AI works, decide the handling of these six notes yourself in `Estimate/Calibration.md`: KH-011, KH-015, KH-020, KH-036, KH-038, KH-040.

Times in the notes are Zulu (Z) or local (L). Local time is UTC plus three hours. [[Reading the reports]] explains the report types and terms.
